The Burj Khalifa is a skyscraper in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. With a total height of 829.8m and a roof height of 828m, it is the world's tallest structure. It has also held the record of the tallest building in the world since its topping out in 2009, surpassing Taipei 101, the holder of that status since 2004.

  • Located in: Burj Park
  • Departments: Burj Khalifa Pool Annex
  • Address: 1 Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Blvd - Downtown Dubai - Dubai - United Arab Emirates
  • Height: 828 m, 830 m to tip CTBUH
  • Floors: 163
  • Owner: Emaar Properties
  • Cost: US$1.5 billion
  • Opened: 4 January 2010; 15 years ago
  • Architects: Adrian Smith, Marshall Strabala, George J. Efstathiou>

The Dubai Miracle Garden is a flower garden located in the district of Dubailand, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. The garden was launched on Valentine's Day in 2013.
